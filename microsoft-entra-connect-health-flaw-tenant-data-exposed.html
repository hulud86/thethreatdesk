Attackers exploited an Entra Connect Health flaw to read Microsoft 365 tenant details

Microsoft says the bug exposed admin email addresses, tenant IDs and license details, but no passwords or tokens. It was introduced in July and fixed server-side on November 19.

By The Threat DeskNovember 21, 2026

Microsoft has told customers that attackers exploited a flaw in the Entra Connect Health service to get read-only access to information about their Microsoft 365 and Entra tenants.

What was exposed

According to Microsoft's notice, the exposed data included:

  • Email addresses of administrators and technical contacts.
  • Tenant IDs, display names, initial domains and country or region.
  • License tier and directory sync status, including the last sync time.
  • Connect Health onboarding status, and an expired certificate from a service retired in late 2024.

Microsoft says no passwords, password hashes, tokens, valid keys, on-premises network details or Azure subscription data were exposed. The flaw could also have allowed changes to monitoring settings, but the company says it found none.

Timeline

The bug was introduced on July 23 and discovered on November 9. Microsoft deployed a server-side fix on November 19 and notified affected customers on November 20. It has not said who carried out the attacks or how many organizations were hit. More than a dozen administrators posted notices online, and some managed service providers received notices for several tenants.

Why it matters

The data is not enough to log in, but it gives attackers a ready-made list of who runs each tenant. That is useful for targeted phishing aimed at the people with the most access.

Who's affected

Organizations using Microsoft Entra and Entra Connect Health, and managed service providers that run multiple tenants.

What to do

No patch is needed, but warn exposed admins about targeted phishing, require phishing-resistant MFA on privileged accounts, review Connect Health settings and Entra sign-in and audit logs, and check any notice in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
