Operation Endgame takes down Gootloader, CastleRAT and StealC servers

Police seized 235 servers and 257 domains tied to three malware families and recovered about 6.4 million stolen logins belonging to more than 40,000 victims.

By The Threat DeskNovember 13, 2026

Police in Europe and the U.S. have hit three malware operations in the latest round of Operation Endgame, The Hacker News and SecurityWeek report. Across Gootloader, CastleRAT and StealC, authorities seized 235 servers and 257 domains. Germany's BKA says about 6.4 million stolen logins belonging to more than 40,000 victims were recovered. No arrests were announced.

The targets

  • Gootloader spreads through fake legal documents planted on hacked websites, often WordPress, that are pushed up in search results. It then downloads other malware, frequently ransomware. The U.S. Justice Department says it has infected at least 25,000 computers since 2018 and was used against at least 44 U.S. ransomware victims, with about $28.8 million in losses. Police also cleaned malware from hundreds of hacked sites.
  • CastleRAT is a remote access trojan known since 2025. Police seized 120 servers and 39 domains.
  • StealC is an infostealer whose operators had rebuilt after a June takedown, partly on the bulletproof host Virtualine, which was shut down in late October.

Check your exposure

Dutch police run a Check your hack service where you can enter an email address to see whether it appears in the seized data. Police warn they never send emails with links, so messages claiming otherwise are scams.

Who's affected

People whose logins were stolen by these malware families, and owners of hacked WordPress sites used to spread Gootloader.

What to do

Check your email address at the Dutch police Check your hack service. Change passwords and turn on MFA for important accounts. WordPress owners should rotate admin passwords, remove unknown accounts and update everything.
